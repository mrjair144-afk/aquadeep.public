🔵 What is Argo?

Argo is a global array of free-drifting profiling floats that measure temperature and salinity of the upper 2000 m of ocean. India's contribution is managed by INCOIS (Indian National Centre for Ocean Information Services), Hyderabad.

How Argo Floats Work

1
Deployment

Floats are deployed from research vessels or aircraft.

2
Drift at 1000 m

Drifts with ocean currents for about 10 days.

3
Deep Profile

Descends to 2000 m collecting temperature and salinity.

4
Ascent

Rises to the surface, sampling the full water column.

5
Satellite Transmission

Sends data via Argos/Iridium to INCOIS servers.

🌊

Float Structure

About 1.5 m tall and 25 kg. Holds a pressure sensor, CTD instrument, battery pack, hydraulic system and satellite antenna.

🛰️

Satellite Communication

Data goes to the Argos system run by CLS France, then to INCOIS Hyderabad for quality control and global distribution via GTS.

🐟

Value for Fishermen

Argo data improves SST forecasts, Potential Fishing Zone identification, cyclone intensity prediction and monsoon forecasting.

📡

India's Argo Program

India operates 70+ floats in the Indian Ocean under the Ministry of Earth Sciences.

🌡️

Temperature Profiles

Each float gives a vertical profile from the surface to 2000 m, revealing the thermocline, mixed layer depth and ocean heat content.

🗺️

Indian Ocean Coverage

Floats cover the Bay of Bengal, Arabian Sea and southern Indian Ocean, supporting PFZ advisories.

Data Source: INCOIS. Official portal: www.incois.gov.in · Argo India: argo.incois.gov.in

❓ Help & User Guide

How to use the dashboard, for researchers, fishermen and ocean enthusiasts.

🗺️

Interactive Argo Float Map

  • Click a marker for WMO ID, position, temperature, salinity, depth.
  • Cyan = Active, Orange = Surfaced, Purple = Deep drift.
  • Zoom with the scroll wheel or map controls.
🤖

AI Ocean Assistant

  • Type a question and press Enter.
  • Click 🎤 to speak.
  • Change language from the dropdown.
📊

Data Visualizations

  • Temperature depth profile
  • Monthly SST bar chart
  • Temperature vs depth scatter
  • Salinity pie chart
  • Potential Fishing Zone chart

For Fishermen (மீனவர்களுக்கு)
